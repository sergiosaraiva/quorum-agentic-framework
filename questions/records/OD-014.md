EXAMPLE RECORD (invented "Acme Orders" domain).

## Decision

Invoice numbers are allocated from a counter row per year, incremented with `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` inside the issuing transaction (TQ-001 option 1). This is gap-free by construction and serialises only invoice issuance. A database sequence (gaps on rollback, rejected by BQ-002) and an external numbering service are not used. VS-002 builds on this.
