## Decision

Answers TQ-014 with its option 1. The application does not log the customer-name query value. The three surfaces of OD-012 are enforced where they exist, per deployment target, in infrastructure code (never by hand in a console):

1. Proxy and access logs omit or redact the `customerName` query value.
2. Telemetry `url.query` (and any equivalent full-URL attribute) omits or redacts the `customerName` value.
3. Database log settings do not record statements with bound parameters or their values, or redact them.

This is a required item of the infrastructure-engineer operational readiness checklist (`.claude/agents/infrastructure-engineer.md`) and is stated in `src/Infrastructure/CLAUDE.md`. The infrastructure review (`security-engineer`) verifies each surface before the first deployment. Until a deployment target is chosen it stays a deployment follow-up: the infrastructure-engineer records a TQ at target selection that carries the three checks. No vendor or product is named here. An application-level telemetry processor and moving the search to POST are not chosen (OD-012 stands).
