EXAMPLE RECORD (invented "Acme Orders" domain).

## Decision

All money amounts use `numeric(18,2)` with a `char(3)` ISO 4217 currency column; C# uses `decimal`. Rounding follows BC-005 (per line, half away from zero). This applies to orders, invoices, refunds and credit notes.

## Context

Floating point types lose cents; the PostgreSQL `money` type ties formatting to locale settings. Decided on 2026-01-06 by the tech lead after TQ-002.
