EXAMPLE RECORD (invented "Acme Orders" domain).

## Question

If order search matches text against the customer name, what does a match mean: case-insensitive substring, prefix, or whole word (full-text)? The KB does not say, and the result set changes with the choice.

## Options

1. Case-insensitive substring match (recommended: simplest, expected by users; needs no extra extension, index choice deferred).
2. Case-insensitive prefix match (cheaper to index, surprises users who type a middle word).
3. Full-text search (stemming, ranking; a new database feature, no present need).

## Answer

Case-insensitive substring match on the customer name (option 1). Answered by the product owner on 2026-11-19. Recorded as OD-003.
