EXAMPLE RECORD (invented "Acme Orders" domain).

## Question

The per-user rate limit on `GET /api/orders` (OD-018) partitions on the principal's `NameIdentifier` claim, then `sub`; a principal with neither shares one "anonymous" bucket. Which claim does the chosen identity provider issue for a person, and do non-user tokens (client-credentials, service accounts) call the orders API? Such tokens may carry no `NameIdentifier` or `sub` meant as a user id, so every service caller would share one bucket. Whether the JWT bearer handler maps `sub` to `NameIdentifier` depends on its configuration; not confirmed against a real provider.

## Options

1. Decide the claim once the identity provider is chosen, and set it in Options (recommended: a configured claim type, with the shared bucket kept for tokens without it).
2. Partition service tokens by client id as well as users by subject.
3. Leave the NameIdentifier-then-`sub` order as built.

## Answer

Decided in OD-026: option 3 plus a requirement. The partition stays `NameIdentifier` then `sub`; the identity provider must issue one of them for every token that calls the orders API; tokens with neither share one bucket (documented, bounded); each adopter confirms its identity provider and records a TQ if it differs. Answered on 2026-11-20 on the owner's delegation to the session's recommendation.
