## Decision

VS-005 and VS-004 rung R2 (status filter) may be built although their hard dependency VS-001 is still `In progress` in `docs/slices/INDEX.md`, which `docs/slices/CLAUDE.md` forbids ("a slice cannot start while a hard dependency is below `Done`"). Reason: the VS-001 entity, endpoints and migration are already in code. The exception is scoped to these two items only, follows the pattern of OD-006 (which covers VS-004 R1 only) and is not a general loosening of the rule. It does not move VS-001 to `Done`. R2 remains stacked on VS-005 (OD-004) and cannot merge before it.
