## Question

The landed VS-001 list sorts by creation time, newest first (code in src/Api/Features/Orders/OrdersService.cs), but no KB chunk or record says this is the required order. Should search results keep that default, and may the caller choose another sort field?

## Options

1. Keep newest first by creation time as the only order, no caller-selectable sort (recommended: no new contract surface, matches what exists).
2. Add a caller-selectable sort over created date and total.
3. Relevance order when a text query is present, otherwise newest first.

## Answer

Decided in OD-010: option 1, newest first, no caller-selectable sort. The owner (product owner, tech lead and privacy roles) answered on 2026-11-20.
