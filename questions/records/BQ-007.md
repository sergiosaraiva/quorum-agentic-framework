EXAMPLE RECORD (invented "Acme Orders" domain).

## Question

The order search filters by a created-date range given as calendar dates. BC-001 stores state changes with a timestamp but does not say in which time zone a calendar day is counted. Which zone defines the day?

## Options

1. UTC days: `created_at >= from 00:00Z` and `created_at < (to + 1 day) 00:00Z`, both bounds inclusive (recommended: matches how `CreatedAt` is stamped, no per-user setting).
2. The caller's time zone (needs a zone parameter or a user setting).
3. A fixed business time zone (needs a configured zone and a rule for daylight saving).

## Answer

UTC days (option 1). Answered by the product owner on 2026-11-19. Recorded as OD-005.
