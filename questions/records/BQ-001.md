EXAMPLE RECORD (invented "Acme Orders" domain).

## Question

BC-003 sets a 30-day refund window after delivery. Does the same window apply to digital goods, which are "delivered" the moment they are downloaded? The KB is silent on digital goods.

## Options

1. Same 30-day window for all goods (recommended: simplest, no new rule).
2. Shorter window (for example 14 days) for digital goods.
3. No refunds once a download has started.

## Answer

Decided in OD-009: option 1, the same 30-day window for all goods. The owner (product owner, tech lead and privacy roles) answered on 2026-11-20.

- 2026-11-20: answered by the owner as product owner (see OD-009); status set to answered; recorded by documentation-curator.
