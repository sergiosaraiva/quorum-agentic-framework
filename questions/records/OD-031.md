## Decision

All three review round caps are 5:

1. In-session Loop-Back cap: at most 5 re-loops per gate chain (`.claude/agents/_SHARED-PATTERNS.md`, "Loop-Back Rule"); a framework-doc change shares one budget of 5 across both gates.
2. Local AI review: the `--max-rounds` default of `scripts/ai_review_local.py` is 5.
3. GitHub AI review: `MAX_ROUNDS` in `.github/workflows/ai-pr-review.yml` is 5.

Nothing else about the loops changes: after the cap the work stops and a human decides.

## Context

In the session of 2026-11-27 the owner, who holds the product owner, tech lead and privacy roles (OD-022), asked to "increase the review limit to 5 rounds". Decided as tech lead (process and tooling). It changes the earlier cap of 3 stated in the docs; each statement of the cap now cites this record or follows the owner text in `_SHARED-PATTERNS.md`.
