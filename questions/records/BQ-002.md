## Question

If invoice creation fails after a number is reserved, may the number stay unused, or must the sequence have no gaps?

## Answer

No gaps. Auditors require a continuous sequence per calendar year. A failed issuance must not consume a number (answered by the product owner on 2026-01-12, reflected in BC-002).
