## Decision

The owner confirms the seven process rules that the earlier process PR proposed for the trial findings C2, C3, D1, D2 and D7 (`docs/reference/pipeline-trial-findings.md`), as written in the files:

1. Blocker definition and per-rung readiness: a blocker is an open BQ/TQ with no recorded working assumption that the slice's criteria or design cannot proceed without; every other open record is non-blocking and is listed as such in the INDEX Open blockers table; readiness may be per rung (`docs/slices/CLAUDE.md`, "Readiness before build"; chunk `QF-021`).
2. Slice splitting guidance (`docs/slices/IMPLEMENTATION-GUIDE.md`, section 4c).
3. The `Blocked` trigger row: any status moves to `Blocked` when work cannot continue (guide, section 4b).
4. When an OD is mandatory: for a waiver of a rule or gate, a scope change and an accepted risk; an answered BQ/TQ needs an OD only when the choice binds work beyond the question (`recording-decisions` skill, "Record types").
5. The decider owns the subject: the `decider` is the role that owns the subject of the decision, and one person holding several roles answers each record in the role it belongs to (`recording-decisions` skill; root `CLAUDE.md`, Team and Communication).
6. The P1 definition: P1 means the record blocks work, or must be answered before a milestone named in its `## Question` (`questions/README.md`; `recording-decisions` skill).
7. Split versus bundle for options: options that are alternative answers to one question stay in one record; split into records linked by `related` when answering one part does not settle the others, or the parts have different deciders or blocked work (`questions/CLAUDE.md`, "One question per record").
