EXAMPLE RECORD (invented "Acme Orders" domain).

## Decision

Order search and the order list keep the VS-001 visibility: any authenticated caller sees all orders (BQ-006 option 1). Two conditions bind later work (the staff-only premise and the Critical re-rating come from the security-engineer review recorded in BQ-006's History, not from the owner). (1) The orders endpoints use a named authorization policy now, instead of bare `RequireAuthorization()`; the policy and its tests are follow-up code tracked in the slice follow-up PR and are not done by this record. (2) Before any environment where non-staff identities (customers) can obtain a token for the API audience, per-caller visibility (BQ-006 options 2 or 3, needing an owner column and a role model) must be decided in a new question, and the exposure is revisited as Critical from that point (the owner's decision; the security-engineer review rates it Low while all identities are staff).

## Context

Decided on 2026-11-20 by the owner, who holds the product owner, tech lead and privacy roles (as in OD-004), in answer to BQ-006, as product owner.
