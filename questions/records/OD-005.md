## Decision

A calendar date in the created-date filter is a UTC day. `createdFrom` matches `created_at >= from 00:00Z`; `createdTo` matches `created_at < (to + 1 day) 00:00Z`; both bounds are inclusive days.

Decided on 2026-11-19 by the owner in answer to BQ-007. Applies to the VS-004 date-range filter chosen in OD-002.
